Using AI is easy. Building with it is engineering. Here's what that means, in plain terms.

AI engineering is making AI models work inside real products: reliable, useful, and safe for the people who use them. It's applied AI. Researchers build new models. Engineers take models that already exist and use them to solve real problems.

The core skills are prompting, working with APIs, evaluating outputs, retrieval, tools, and data pipelines. You don't need all of them on day one.

Start small. Pick one task you do every week and automate it with a single API call. Then add to it.

Over the coming posts I'll walk through these skills one at a time, with examples you can try yourself.
