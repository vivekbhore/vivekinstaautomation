Chatbots answer questions. AI agents take a goal and work out the steps to reach it.

Generative AI writes, draws or summarizes when you give it a prompt. You ask, it replies, and it's done.

An AI agent goes further. You give it a goal, and it plans, uses a tool, checks the result, and decides the next step. It keeps going until the job is finished or it gets stuck.

Quick test: one reply and done? That's generative AI. Plans, uses tools and loops? That's an agent.

Most agents are built on generative AI. The generative part writes and reasons, and the agent part decides what to do with it.
