Your bag never sees the plane's name, yet it lands on the right one. Here is how.

It starts with a barcode on a paper tag. That number follows your bag through scanners and sorting belts, and it decides which lane the bag takes. Bags for one flight are grouped together, loaded into containers or onto carts, and scanned again as they go into the hold. Each step checks that the bag is headed for the right plane.
