How does an ATM actually work? It's four steps behind a simple screen: you insert your card, enter your PIN, the bank checks your balance, and then the cash comes out.

Your PIN is encrypted the moment you type it, and the machine never stores it. The bank approves every withdrawal before a single note is counted.
