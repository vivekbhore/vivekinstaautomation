You have used ChatGPT, but do you know what the GPT part means? It is simpler than it sounds: a word guesser trained on huge amounts of text.

GPT stands for Generative Pre-trained Transformer. Generative means it creates new text. Pre-trained means it learned from books and websites before you ever type to it. Transformer is the design that lets it link each word to the words it relates to.

At heart, it picks the most likely next word, then guesses again, until the answer is complete. ChatGPT is the chat app you type into. GPT is the engine that writes each reply.

It writes what sounds right, which is not always true. For facts that matter, check a trusted source.
