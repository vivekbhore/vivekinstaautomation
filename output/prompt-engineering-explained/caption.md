Most bad AI answers come from vague questions. Prompt engineering is the fix.

It means writing instructions that steer an AI toward the output you want. Here are the main techniques.

Zero-shot: a clear instruction with no examples. Good for simple tasks.

Few-shot: show two or three examples of the pattern you want, then ask. Good when the format matters.

Chain of thought: ask the model to reason step by step before answering. Good for maths and logic.

Role and context: tell it who to act as and who the answer is for. Good for tone and depth.

Start small, then refine each answer. Which technique do you use most?
